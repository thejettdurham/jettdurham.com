# A little about me.

Heya, I'm Jett Durham and welcome to my little corner of the _world wide web_. I'm a software engineer by trade with expertise across the full web development stack. I'm obsessive about user experience, thoughtful interfaces, and ergonomic workflows.

Outside software I’m a musician, a gamer, and a lover of technology that supports human flourishing. Most of my recent personal projects are in the XR (eXtended Reality) domain.

I currently reside in Wichita, KS with my lovely wife, Amanda, and our three dogs: Gidget, Wheatley, and Atlas.

I'm always tinkering with, learning about, or thinking about something. This is a place where I can share all of that with the world. You can also find me on [GitHub](https://github.com/thejettdurham).
